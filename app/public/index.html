    Alerts and on-call

    Where alerts are sent

    Add as many Teams group chats and personal chats as you like. For each one you choose which alerts it gets, for which sites, and whether only while a certain person is on call. Nothing is sent until a real alert happens or you press a test button.

    How to get a Teams link
    1. In Teams open Workflows and pick the template Send webhook alerts to a chat.
    2. Group chat: for "Post in" choose Group chat and pick the chat.
    3. Personal chat: the person who should receive alerts creates it and chooses Chat with Workflow bot. If Teams shows a Recipient box, enter their email there.
    4. Create it, copy the link at the end, and paste it into the destination here. Each destination needs its own link.
